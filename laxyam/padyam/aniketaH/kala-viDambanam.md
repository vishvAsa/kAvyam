**Verse 1**  
**Sanskrit:**  
ये पेतुः सवितुर्दृशोर्ह विषया आसृष्टिकालात्समान्  
आघस्रं विवदन्ति शास्त्रपरगा यत्नेन निर्नामकाः।  
“पाषण्डोऽसि! न हि त्वमेव पदजो! बौद्धोऽसि! दस्यो! ऽबले!  
मत्पक्षी परमः सतां!” परमिदं चित्रं तु मे रोचते॥१॥

**English:**  
Whatever subjects fell on the eyes of the Sun since the time of creation, all of them are debated about all day by subject matter experts who remain anonymous, with great effort:  
"You’re a heretic! No, you are only a Śūdra! You’re a Buddhist! You dasyu! Woman! Those on my side are supreme among the pious!"  
But this circus delights me.

**Verse 2**  
**Sanskrit:**  
विद्यां बुद्ध्वा पुस्तकस्थाम् अनर्थां  
धीरा बुद्ध्वा वादिनञ् चान्य-जात्यम्।  
हृत्वा ग्रन्थात् पाश्चिमाद् गुप्त-पृष्ठम्  
अन्तर्जाले कूजयित्वा जयन्ति॥२॥

**English:**  
Strong minded people, having understood that 'knowledge which stays in books is useless', and having understood their opponent to be from a different caste, lift an obscure page from a Western book, and win by tweeting it on the internet.

**Verse 3**  
**Sanskrit:**  
यत्नेनातततन्तुभिः प्रचलिताः शत्रोर् विभङ्क्तुं गुणान्  
सत्पत्रा ग्रथिताः खराश् च विषिणो घ्नन्ति स्वकान् धर्मतः।  
“दम्भेनाप्य् अपरं प्रहन्म” इति च व्यूढे अनीके युधे  
किं भूयः कुरु-वंश-सङ्ख्यम्? अयि नो, वादो महाकूजके॥३॥

**English:**  
Those [arrows/snippets] that were released to break the enemy's guṇa-s (bowstring/quality), using carefully strung threads (bowstring/twt threads), having good patra-s (feathers/pages), being grathita (tied/compiled), sharp, poisonous — these are killing people who are one's own(,) per Dharma.  
Two armies have assembled for battle with the motive "we will kill the other even if it is through deceit!"  
Has a war broken out again among the Kuru-s? No no, this is an argument on Twitter.
